Hi — I’m **Leo Villani**, a PhD student in **Statistics** at **Cornell University**.

My interests sit at the intersection of **statistical theory**, **machine learning**, and **representation learning**, with a particular focus on uncertainty, structure, and generalization. I’m especially drawn to problems where theory and computation meaningfully interact.

Previously, I studied **Applied Mathematics** and **Computer Science** at **UC Berkeley**, where I was deeply involved in teaching. I’ve been a GSI for courses including **Calculus II (Math 1B)** and **Linear Algebra & Differential Equations (Math 54)**, and I care a lot about clear explanations, good problem design, and building supportive learning environments.

This site is a living record of my work — research notes, teaching materials, talks, and projects — and a place where I experiment a bit with structure and presentation.
